    The product under overhaul.

    1. About the company

    Siam Commercial Bank, established by royal charter on 30 January 1906 as the first Thai bank. A universal bank serving retail, SME and corporate customers, with operations across Cambodia, China, Myanmar, Vietnam, Singapore and Laos.

    Since 2022 it has sat inside SCBX, a holding company structured in three “generations”: GEN 1 banking, GEN 2 consumer and digital finance, GEN 3 platform and technology. SCB Easy belongs to GEN 1 and is the group’s most visible consumer touchpoint.

    Stated strategic direction is SCB Transformation, prioritising digital channels, organisational agility, data analytics and ecosystem links. Detail in [[who-uses-scb-easy]].

    2. About the product

    The bank’s retail mobile banking app and its only self-service retail channel — SCB retired SCB Easy Net, its internet banking service, directing customers to the app.

    That single fact sets the stakes for this entire audit. Anyone who cannot use the app cannot self-serve at all.

    Published feature set, positioning and the staleness of its storefront are covered in [[what-scb-easy-offers]]. What people actually do with it: 75% of transactions were fund transfers as of 2020 — see [[the-core-use-case]].

    Retrieved 2026-09-17 from the Thailand storefronts.

    Supporting iOS 14 is the notable technical fact. Every competitor requires iOS 15. Read next to the digital-exclusion evidence in [[demographics]], it looks like a deliberate inclusion choice — and it constrains whatever the overhaul builds. Confirm whether it is intentional or inherited.

    Release cadence looks thin. The latest release note is a single line about transaction history under the investment tile, while Bangkok Bank and ttb touch shipped five substantive items each. One release is not a pattern; ten would be. Not yet collected.

    4. Key screens

    All 46 SCB Easy screens from the prior inventory are on this page, filed by archetype.

    Read the captions. The inventory’s author recorded observations, not labels — “balance right-aligned and unlabelled”, “11 favourites as identical letter avatars”, “15-cell grid, one cell disabled, no search”, “the only search in the app”. Those are audit findings already, and they are the richest evidence in this study.

    Still to capture, by archetype:

    • Login and onboarding — 2 screens exist across the entire prior inventory
    • Transfer and confirmation — the tier 1 journey
    • Error, empty and offline states
    • The AI Advisory Chatbot surface
    • Cross-app handoff into PointX or INVX

    5. Design breakdown

    Not yet assessable. Requires real interface captures, which do not exist for most of the app. Scaffolded so findings file consistently:

    Illustration — house style, where used, whether it carries meaning or decorates.

    Assets — iconography set, photography treatment, brand marks in-product.

    Design atmosphere — the overall register. Formal or familiar, dense or airy, cautious or confident. Whether it reads as a bank, a wallet, or a shopping app.

    Colour — the palette in use, how far it strays from SCB purple, how colour carries meaning for state and for money in and out, and whether contrast clears WCAG AA.

    App UI — navigation model and depth, component vocabulary, the type scale, and Thai script handling specifically: line-height against Latin, font pairing, numeral treatment.

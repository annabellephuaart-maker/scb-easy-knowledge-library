    SCB Sister Apps

    Status

    Type

    Three of the nine SCBX consumer apps, audited to test whether problems are product-specific or group-wide.

    Why these three

    CardX, INVX and PointX, selected from the nine-app portfolio.

    Together they cover all three SCBX business pillars and three different relationships with SCB Easy: CardX was carved out of SCB’s own card business, INVX is the investment arm, and PointX is the loyalty layer that SCB Easy links into. If a design language is going to span the group, these are the three that would have to hold it.

    What this group is for

    One question: is there anything holding these products together visually or behaviourally, other than ownership?

    The prior inventory found cross-app handoff appearing exactly once across 145 screens. Nine apps, three pillars, one group, and one observable moment of connective tissue. That is the strongest argument in the evidence base for a shared design language, and it is also the thing these three pages should confirm or overturn.

    What to look for, consistently

    • Shared anything. Typeface, colour, spacing, component shapes, iconography, tone.
    • Divergence that is deliberate — a lending app leaning warm and reassuring while an investment app leans dense and technical is a legitimate choice. Divergence that is merely accidental is the problem.
    • The handoff itself. What happens when a user moves from SCB Easy into PointX or INVX. Is there continuity, or does the user arrive somewhere that feels like another company?
    • Whether the better-rated apps do something different. INVX and PointX rate 4.8 and 4.7 on Play against SCB Easy’s 4.3. Narrower scope explains some of that, but not necessarily all.

    Evidence ceiling

    Store ratings compare sentiment, not usability, and these are Play figures against a flagship whose iOS rating tells a different story. A visual comparison shows what each team decided, never whether users noticed or cared.

    Open questions

    • OQ: Does SCBX have a stated position on cross-app consistency, or is divergence deliberate?
    • OQ: Do any of these apps share a component library or token set today?
    • OQ: What does the SCB Easy to PointX handoff actually look like?

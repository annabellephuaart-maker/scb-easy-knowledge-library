    About us

    Siam Commercial Bank was established by royal charter on 30 January 1906 as the first Thai bank. It operates today as a universal bank serving retail, SME and corporate customers, with an international presence across Cambodia, China, Myanmar, Vietnam, Singapore and Laos, and subsidiaries including SCB Securities and SCB Asset Management.

    Retail services span home loans, personal credit, car hire purchase, cards, currency exchange, remittances, investment and insurance. Corporate and SME services cover cash management, lending, trade finance, treasury, capital markets and advisory.

    The bank's stated strategic direction is SCB Transformation, which prioritises digital channels, organisational agility, data analytics capability, and ecosystem links between consumers and entrepreneurs.

    Summarised from scb.co.th/en/about-us. That page is the source of truth; this is a précis and may lag it.

    About SCB Easy

    SCB Easy is the bank's retail mobile banking app and its primary consumer digital channel. SCB retired its SCB Easy Net internet banking service, making the app the main route for self-service retail banking.

    Its published feature set covers four areas:

    • Money movement — fee-free domestic transfers to any Thai bank, bill payment by QR or provider, and top-ups for mobile credit, tolls, e-wallets and prepaid cards.
    • Account access — account management, transaction history, and cardless ATM withdrawal with no transaction fee.
    • Financial products — WEALTH4U investment advisory, EASY-D debenture accounts, and the SCB Gold Marketplace.
    • Lifestyle and security — "Just for You" personalisation, "Easy Bonus" offers, and Easy Security. Registration is by facial recognition, SCB ATM, or branch.

    Summarised from scb.co.th · SCB Easy.
